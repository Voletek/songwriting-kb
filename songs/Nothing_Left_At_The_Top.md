## PRODUCTION NOTES:

- **Key:** A minor, modulating to C major (relative major) at the Final Chorus — A minor carries the hollow-depletion affect (mode is the #1 emotional cue); the lift to the relative major is a soft honest-breath release, not a fake-triumph jump, which is exactly why the relative major (no key-signature change, same notes recontextualized) is chosen over a bright modulation up.
- **Tempo:** 74 BPM (Adagio–Andante border) — inside the Formula 1 Vulnerability band (70–85); weary and dragging without tipping into dirge.
- **Groove/Time-Feel:** Straight, laid-back; brushed backbeat entering only from the first chorus. Near-rubato feel in the bridge.
- **Time Signature:** 4/4.
- **Chord Progression (Nashville / actual):**
  - Verse: i – VI – III – VII (Am – F – C – G) — circling, unresolved autopilot.
  - Pre-Chorus: iv – VI – III – V (Dm – F – C – E) — the E (V) pulls tension toward the question.
  - Chorus: i – VI – III – VII (Am – F – C – G) — same spine as the verse, restrained, the confession sits in the minor.
  - Bridge: iv – i – VI – V (Dm – Am – F – E) — stripped, exposed, hanging on the V with no resolution.
  - Final Chorus (C major): I – vi – IV – V (C – Am – F – G) — the same chords now heard from C as home; the old i (Am) becomes vi, so the hollow chord is now a passing color inside major, not the gravity center. This is the whole point of the modulation: nothing new is added, the same weight is simply re-heard as release.
- **Modulation:** A minor → C major, entering on the Final Chorus via a `[Key Change: A minor to C major]` tag; relative-major pivot (shared tones), reserved as the emotional payoff.
- **Vocal:** Solo mid-low male, close-mic, breathy, conversational, slight cracks. Whispered-weary verses → strained (not belted) choruses → fuller and open final chorus → quiet and unguarded outro. Faint East Tennessee lilt surfaces only on the rawest lines (the bridge, "I'm already halfway there").
- **Instruments (by prominence):** Felt piano, fingerpicked electric guitar, warm bass, brushed/minimal drums, ambient pads, distant strings, room tone throughout.
- **Dynamics (section-by-section):** Intro pp → Verse 1 soft → Pre-Chorus crescendo → Chorus full (restrained) → Verse 2 soft (fuller texture, strings) → Pre-Chorus crescendo → Chorus full → Bridge pp (stripped, near-silence, exposed vocal) → Final Chorus swell (layered, C major lift) → Outro diminuendo → fade out.
- **Hook Type:** Lyric/title confession hook — "Got nothing left inside of me" / "too tired to want it now." Placed as the payoff of the first chorus; the hook signal arrives ~15–20s into the first sung vocal, per the brief.
- **Rhyme Scheme:**
  - Verse 1: ABBA (again/blink → sink/when as near-rhyme frame; slant throughout).
  - Pre-Chorus: AABB (tide/behind slant, stop/drop).
  - Chorus: AABB then AABB (here/disappear, key/me; now/how, let go/know).
  - Verse 2: ABBA (fine/behind-guy slant frame; car/are).
  - Bridge: near-rhyme/free (dead/said-it/meant/there) — deliberately loosened so the turn reads as speech, not craft.
  - Final Chorus: mirrors the Chorus scheme with 1–2 lines re-rhymed (here/disappear, key/me; now/how, let go/go).
- **Emotional Arc:** Numb autopilot at the top of a hollow "win" builds through the three-voices pressure into a bridge that stops asking "which door?" and admits the exhaustion was the answer all along, so the final chorus turns the same confession into the first honest breath — relief in admitting it, not solving it.
