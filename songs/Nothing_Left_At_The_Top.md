## PRODUCTION NOTES:

- **Key:** A minor, modulating to C major (relative major) at the Final Chorus — A minor carries the anxious-dread-and-dissociation affect (mode is the #1 emotional cue). The lift to the relative major on the final chorus is one honest breath / a decision to leave, NOT a fake-triumph jump; the relative major is chosen precisely because it shares the same notes (no key-signature change) so the payoff reads as the *same* feeling re-heard as release, not a brightening.
- **Tempo:** ~95 BPM base (anxious mid-tempo, off the sleepy ballad). **Internal tempo-change device:** the song runs on a steady anxious 95 through Verse 1 / Pre-Chorus 1 / Chorus 1 / Verse 2, then **Pre-Chorus 2 ACCELERATES** (`[Feel: Accelerando]` into `[Feel: Double-Time]`) as the panic runs away from the narrator; the **Bridge DROPS to near-silence** (`[Feel: Rubato]`, drums/synth/bass out) — the hollow center — before the Final Chorus swells back in on the key lift.
- **Groove/Time-Feel:** Straight but driving-yet-restrained; heartbeat kick under the verses, fuller kit from the chorus, a ticking motif threading anxiety from the intro. Pre-Chorus 2 pushes into double-time; the bridge is near-rubato and nearly beatless.
- **Time Signature:** 4/4.
- **Chord Progression (Nashville / actual):**
  - Verse: i – VI – III – VII (Am – F – C – G) — circling, unresolved, dissociated autopilot.
  - Pre-Chorus: iv – VI – III – V (Dm – F – C – E) — the E (V) pulls tension toward the panic; in Pre-Chorus 2 the same shape is played double-time to run away.
  - Chorus: i – VII – VI – V (Am – G – F – E) — a descending pull that keeps sinking, tension held on the V, never resolving to a "win."
  - Bridge (near-silent): i – iv – VI – V (Am – Dm – F – E) — stripped to piano/vocal, exposed, hanging on the V with no resolution.
  - Final Chorus (C major): I – V – vi – IV (C – G – Am – F) — the same pitches now heard from C as home; the old i (Am) becomes vi, so the hollow chord is a passing color inside major, not the gravity center. Nothing new is added; the same weight is re-heard as release.
- **Modulation:** A minor → C major, entering on the Final Chorus via a `[Key Change: A minor to C major]` tag; relative-major pivot (shared tones), reserved as the emotional payoff.
- **Vocal:** Solo mid-low male, close-mic, breathy, cracks left in. Dissociated/clipped verses (watching himself) → breathless, running pre-choruses → fuller strained (not belted) choruses → spoken/broken bridge → fuller-and-open first-honest-breath final chorus → quiet, unguarded outro. Faint East Tennessee lilt surfaces only on the rawest lines (the bridge, "I'm already fucking halfway there").
- **Instruments (by prominence):** Felt piano, pulsing/anxious synth + ticking motif, driving-but-restrained drums over a heartbeat kick, warm bass, ambient pads, distant strings, room tone throughout.
- **Dynamics (section-by-section):** Intro pp (piano, room tone, ticking) → Verse 1 soft (heartbeat kick, bass enter) → Pre-Chorus 1 crescendo (synth clamps in) ↑ → Chorus 1 full (drums, pads) → Verse 2 soft (strings, dread returns) → **Pre-Chorus 2 crescendo + ACCELERANDO → double-time** ↑↑ (panic runs away) → Chorus 2 full → **Bridge pp / rubato / near-silent** ↓↓ (drums, synth, bass, strings OUT — hollow center) → Final Chorus swell on the C-major lift ↑ (piano, bass, pads, strings return) → Outro diminuendo → fade out.
- **Hook Type:** Lyric/title confession hooks — "I want to disappear" (the payoff line closing every chorus) and the dissociation image "watching from the ceiling, watching someone else." The hook signal lands early — within the first chorus, ~15–20s into the first sung vocal after a short intro — and is textable.
- **Rhyme Scheme:**
  - Intro: free/near-rhyme (name/loud, part/me) — deliberately unmoored, spoken panic.
  - Verse 1: loose ABCB (day/neck/remember/yet slant; morning/won/even/run) — clipped, dissociated.
  - Pre-Chorus: AABB-ish (now/bow, white/slow near-rhyme, go/go) — accelerating.
  - Chorus: AABB then AABB (else/else, grin/skin; say/away, world/disappear near-rhyme) — the last line intentionally hangs off-rhyme so "disappear" lands unresolved.
  - Verse 2: AABB (guess/rest, say/away; shoulder/land slant, maybes/stand) — imposter-fear plain-spoken.
  - Pre-Chorus 2: mirrors PC1 escalated (wrong/long, door/anymore, no) — deliberately frayed.
  - Bridge: near-rhyme/free (dead/said-it/meant/there; maybes/up/out/us) — loosened so the turn reads as broken speech, not craft.
  - Final Chorus: mirrors the Chorus scheme with the meaning-shifted lines re-cast (else/else, grin/in; stay/away, world/disappear) — recognizably the same chorus.
- **Emotional Arc:** A career milestone triggers a mild panic attack — dissociative dread and imposter-syndrome fear tightening until the panic runs away with him, then the bridge drops the floor out and he admits the fraud-fear was eating him alive, so the final chorus turns "I want to disappear" from terror into a decision — honest uncertainty, the first real breath, walking out without knowing what's next.
