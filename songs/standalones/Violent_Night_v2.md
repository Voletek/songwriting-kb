## LYRICS:

[Title: Violent Night]

[Production Direction: Dark Nordic Yule folk waltz in 3/4, two lilting stresses per bar when sung. 16-bar instrumental intro on lyre, tagelharpa and distant iron bells. Close, sparse verses. War drums and strings enter at each pre-chorus, and choruses are stomping chants in a wide hall. Every waltz break restates the intro theme, heavier each time, and the climax brings the original melody back at full force. Bridge strips to cello drone and low voice. Final chorus is the biggest moment. Closing refrain warms into F major, then the outro returns to D minor on bare lyre, one drum strike and a held bowed note. No bright Christmas sparkle.]

[Vocal Direction: Deep gravelly bass-baritone storyteller, slight Scandinavian lilt, rolled r's. ## PRODUCTION NOTES

```
• Key: D minor — minor mode carries the dread and the old-world menace, and D minor
  sits low in a bass-baritone's chest so the storyteller sounds weathered, not
  strained. The closing refrain (Breakdown) moves to the relative major, F, for the
  morning and the gifts, cued with [Key Change: Relative major, F]. The outro returns
  to D minor so the last bell and the whispered "ho ho ho" stay unsettling.

• Tempo: 132 BPM in 3/4 (one bar ≈ 1.36 s). Sung sections carry two lilting stresses
  per bar, about 2 bars (≈2.7 s) per line, like a swaying 6/8. The one-in-a-bar
  waltz pulse belongs to the instrumental breaks. The double-time climax gives the
  "frantic waltz" without changing the tempo marking.

• Time Signature: 3/4 — the waltz is the song's identity, and the intro theme
  returns in every instrumental break.

• Chord Progression (Nashville / actual in D minor):
  Intro / Interludes: i – VII – VI – VII (Dm – C – B♭ – C) — the waltz theme
  Verse:        i – VI – III – VII, cadence v – i (Dm – B♭ – F – C, Am – Dm)
  Pre-Chorus:   iv – VI – VII – V (Gm – B♭ – C – A) — harmonic-minor A pulls hard
                into the chorus
  Chorus:       i – VII – VI – V (Dm – C – B♭ – A) on the chant lines;
                III – VII – iv – V (F – C – Gm – A) on "Raise your cup";
                i – VII – i (Dm – C – Dm) to close
  Bridge:       D cello drone under iv – i (Gm – Dm) on "He knows"; VI – VII – i
                (B♭ – C – Dm) on "So leave a cup"
  Build:        i pedal under VI – VII – V (B♭ – C – A), rising
  Final Chorus: as chorus; last cadence held on V (A) under "WIDE AWAKE", then
                resolving
  Breakdown:    I – V – vi – IV (F – C – Dm – B♭) — relative-major warmth
  Outro:        i – VII – VI – i (Dm – C – B♭ – Dm), ending on an open D fifth

• Vocal: Deep gravelly male bass-baritone, old storyteller with a slight
  Scandinavian lilt and rolled r's. Close and dry in the verses, playful menace in
  Verse 2, full-throated chant with layered male gang vocals in a wide hall on every
  chorus, low near-whisper over the cello drone in the bridge, warm in the
  Breakdown, almost whispered on the final "ho ho ho".

• Instruments (by prominence):
  1. Bowed lyre (intro theme, outro solo)
  2. Tagelharpa (drone and rhythmic bowing in the breaks)
  3. War drums / ceremonial drums (pre-choruses, build, climax)
  4. Frame drums (chorus stomp)
  5. Low bowed strings (pre-choruses, breaks, build)
  6. Deep cello drone (bridge, fills the low register)
  7. Iron bells and slow-shaken iron sleigh bells (intro, colour throughout)
  8. Layered male gang vocals (choruses)

• Dynamics:
  Intro:         pp — 16 bars of lyre, tagelharpa, distant bells ↗
  Verse 1:       p — close fireside storytelling ↗
  Pre-Chorus:    p → mf — drums and strings enter ↗↗
  Chorus 1:      f — stomping chant ↗↗↗
  Interlude:     mf — waltz theme, steady stomp ↘
  Verse 2:       mp — rhythmic menace ↗
  Pre-Chorus:    mf → f ↗↗
  Chorus 2:      ff ↗↗↗
  Interlude:     f — strings carry the theme ↘↗
  Bridge:        p — drums and strings out, cello drone and low voice ↘↘↘
  Build:         p → ff — ceremonial drums and strings rise ↗↗↗
  Climax:        ff — double-time frantic waltz, original melody returns ↗↗↗↗
  Final Chorus:  full impact — biggest moment ↗↗↗↗
  Breakdown:     mp — F major, drums out ↘↘
  Outro:         pp — D minor, solitary lyre, whisper, one drum strike, held note ↘↘↘

• Hook Type: Title hook built on a pun ("Violent Night" against "Silent Night"),
  delivered as a chant hook. It opens and closes every chorus. Placement: first
  chorus about 44 s after the first sung word, around 1:05 of track time (16-bar
  intro + 12-line verse + pre-chorus). Late for pop, acceptable for a cinematic
  story song.

• Rhyme Scheme:
  Verses: ABCB ballad stanzas (low/go, sky/high, bone/known) — closed, steady,
          storyteller's rhythm
  Pre-Chorus: ABCB (door/more, roar/four) — closes but leans forward on the drums
  Chorus: XAXA chant (RINGING/SINGING), AABB couplets (flame/name, gold/cold), then
          back to NIGHT/BRIGHT — strongly closed, built for a crowd
  Final Chorus tag: XAXA with a new perfect rhyme on a stressed syllable
          (SHAKE/AWAKE), calling back to "NO ONE SLEEPS"
  Bridge: ABCB (done/none perfect, deer/near) — closed; the turn to "you" lands
          on the first line
  Build: XAXA (snow/go) — "nowhere he can't go" answers "where mortal men don't go"
  Breakdown / Outro: XAXA (gone/on, bright/night) — settled, the night closing

• Emotional Arc: A village bars its doors against a red-cloaked king out of the old
  Yule, learns he judges by deeds — a toy for one child, a battleaxe for the thief,
  remembrance for the child who went hungry — and wakes to gifts in the snow while
  the bell keeps ringing somewhere beyond the pines.

• Formula: Neither house formula. A seasonal story ballad that borrows Formula 2's
  chant chorus and minor-to-major lift but keeps a waltz pulse and long
  instrumental breaks.
```
