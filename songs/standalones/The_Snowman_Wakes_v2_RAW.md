[Production Direction — Preserve the Storms of the North melodic identity and Nordic instrumentation. Heavy, relentless 6/8 war stomp. Open at full power with enormous war horns, thunderous drums, frozen wind, and deep wordless male chanting. Drop suddenly into an unsettling nursery-rhyme verse. Progressively intensify the percussion, bowed strings, and vocal menace. The chorus should sound like an entire mountain walking. During the bridge, remove nearly everything except a deep drone and close, intimate whisper. Final chorus becomes overwhelming. Outro returns to the innocent nursery-rhyme melody, now horribly distorted. No children's voices, sleigh bells, or modern pop elements.]

[Vocal Direction — One impossibly deep male bass, cavernous and ancient, with a cold Scandinavian inflection. The singer is the frost giant himself, pretending to narrate an innocent children's tale. Verses are disturbingly playful, almost delighted. Choruses are commanding, rhythmic, and monstrous, with layered low male harmonies. In the bridge, his voice becomes intimate, amused, and quietly cruel. No high male vocals, theatrical cartoon monster voices, or death-metal growls.]
