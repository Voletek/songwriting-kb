# VIOLENT NIGHT
*Dark Nordic Yule Folk — 5:59*

[Extended Instrumental Intro — Haunting Nordic Waltz, Bowed Lyre, Tagelharpa, Distant Winter Bells]

[Verse 1 — Deep, Gravelly Male Baritone, Dark Storytelling]

The snow lay thick upon the roofs
The village fires burned low
When something stirred beyond the pines
Where mortal men don't go

Eight great beasts with antlers black
Came thundering through the sky
And on a sleigh of ancient oak
A bearded king rode high

His cloak was red as winter blood
His beard was white as bone
And carved upon his iron belt
Were names of those he'd known

[Pre-Chorus — Low War Drums Enter, Bowed Strings Rise]

The children heard the sleigh bells ring
The old men barred the door
For Yule had brought its wanderer
To walk the earth once more

[Chorus — Thunderous Nordic Folk, Powerful Baritone, Layered Male Harmonies]

OH, VIOLENT NIGHT!
THE BELLS ARE RINGING!
THROUGH THE FROZEN SKY
THE YULE KING'S SINGING!

Raise your cup and feed the flame
Let the winter know your name
For the good, a gift of gold
For the wicked, something cold

OH, VIOLENT NIGHT!
THE STARS BURN BRIGHT!
NO ONE SLEEPS
ON A VIOLENT NIGHT!

[Instrumental — Original Waltz Theme, Heavy Frame Drums, Rhythmic Tagelharpa]

[Verse 2 — Rhythmic Baritone, Playful Menace]

He climbed down through the chimney smoke
With frost upon his beard
The youngest child crept from his bed
And asked the thing he'd feared

"Have you brought us toys, old man?
Or swords to fight the snow?"
The wanderer gave a crooked grin
And answered, "Both, you know."

He set a wooden horse beside
A little woolen bear
Then pulled a rusted battleaxe
From somewhere in the air

"For those who kept their promises
There's sweetness in my sack
But those who stole their neighbor's bread
May find I'm coming back."

[Pre-Chorus — Faster Percussion, Rising Folk Melody]

The rooftops shook, the ravens cried
The storm began to roar
And somewhere down the village road
A man confessed to four

[Chorus — Full Nordic Percussion, Strong Melodic Hook]

OH, VIOLENT NIGHT!
THE BELLS ARE RINGING!
THROUGH THE FROZEN SKY
THE YULE KING'S SINGING!

Raise your cup and feed the flame
Let the winter know your name
For the good, a gift of gold
For the wicked, something cold

OH, VIOLENT NIGHT!
THE STARS BURN BRIGHT!
NO ONE SLEEPS
ON A VIOLENT NIGHT!

[Extended Instrumental — Spiraling Nordic Waltz, Bowed String Melodies, War Drums]

[Bridge — Instruments Pull Back, Low Ominous Baritone]

They say he keeps a book of names
Bound in weathered hide
And every year he reads it through
While ravens watch beside

He knows the lies you whispered
He knows the wrong you've done
He knows the child who shared his bread
When there was barely some

So leave a cup upon the hearth
And something for the deer
For if you hear those iron bells
The Yule King may be near

[Build — Deep Ceremonial Drums, Rising Strings, Increasing Intensity]

Hear the hooves upon the mountain!
Hear the bells across the snow!
Lock your doors or light your hearthfires!
There's nowhere he can't go!

Through the storm and through the darkness
Over valley, hill, and sea
The old king rides before the dawn
To see what he shall see!

[Instrumental Climax — Frantic Nordic Waltz, Powerful Drums, Original Melody Returns]

[Final Chorus — Full-Throated Baritone, Layered Viking Harmonies]

OH, VIOLENT NIGHT!
THE BELLS ARE RINGING!
THROUGH THE FROZEN SKY
THE YULE KING'S SINGING!

Raise your cup and feed the flame
Let the winter know your name
For the good, a gift of gold
For the wicked, something cold

OH, VIOLENT NIGHT!
LET THE HEAVENS SHAKE!
THE KING OF YULE
RIDES BEFORE DAYBREAK!

[Final Refrain — Folk Percussion Fades, Warm Baritone]

And when the morning paints the snow
And all the stars are gone
The children find their treasures
As the old king travels on

[Outro — Gentle Opening Waltz Theme, Solitary Bowed Lyre]

The snow lay thick upon the roofs
The village fires burned bright
And somewhere far beyond the pines
A bell rang through the night

[Low Baritone — Almost Whispered]

Ho... ho... ho...

[Deep Drum Strike — Final Bowed Note]

[End]
