## Calibration & Technical Audit

- **Self-Score Delta:** The song was written and critiqued in the same session. SOP 02 notes self-critiques inflate by ~1.0, so treat 7.8 as a ceiling, not a floor.
- **Layer Audit:** No named () layers; the ALL CAPS "THUMP" lines are the only inline emphasis (a v5.5-era technique, harmless on V6).
- **Production-Lyric Alignment:** Aligned. Stomps drop out for the whispered bridge and return for the rampage, matching the bed's lull (~1:35-2:05) and climax.
- **Discovery Depth:** MEDIUM. The stolen hat (the theft that wakes it) and the shared "little ones" between the narrator and the giant reward a second listen; making the narrator explicitly the giant would add a layer.

## Strongest Line

> "One gave a hat that was not her own" — It does three jobs in nine syllables. It's the Frosty nod (the magic hat) without borrowing a word, it plants a small guilt that makes the waking feel earned instead of random, and it lands as the last, slightly wrong line of an otherwise sweet nursery-rhyme quatrain. The runner-up, "The hill stands up and it walks to town", gives the giant its scale in one image.

## Flagged Issues

1. **No hook signal before ~1:08**
   Current: the intro is instrumental and Verse 1 runs ~40 s before the first "THUMP".
   Why: 52 s from the first sung word to the hook is past the 5-30 s window, and 0:00-0:30 is the likeliest skip zone (Skip Test). The bed fixes the chorus position, so the signal has to come earlier in other words.
   Fix:
   a) Add a whispered "Thump — thump — thump" at the end of the Intro (`[Vocal: Whisper]`). It teases the hook at ~0:12; the risk is that Suno starts singing earlier and shortens the intro, which is fine for a radio target.
   b) Add a whispered "Thump — thump — thump" between the two Verse 1 quatrains, at the "something opened its eyes" turn (~0:36).

2. **Narrator and giant aren't tied together**
   Current: Vocal Direction describes a storyteller, then "the giant himself" in the bridge.
   Why: one singer does both roles, so the POV flip reads as a delivery change only. The shared "little ones" already hints the storyteller is the giant; saying so makes the bridge a real reveal (Moore: persona).
   Fix:
   a) Vocal Direction: "The storyteller is the giant all along, telling the children's story back to them; the bridge is where he stops pretending."
   b) Leave the lyrics unchanged; the double reading needs no new words.

3. **Generic pre-chorus line**
   Current: "A breath that was colder than the night"
   Why: "colder than the night" is a stock comparison in a song whose other images are specific.
   Fix:
   a) "A sigh that frosted the lantern light" (9), an image the villagers would see.
   b) "A breath that whitened every pane" (8).

4. **Generic chorus line**
   Current: "Every door is barred tonight"
   Why: true but flat; it's the one chorus line with no picture of cold.
   Fix:
   a) "Every latch is frozen tight" (7): the doors can't be opened, so the fear and the frost are one image; it still rhymes with "white".
   b) "Mothers bar the doors tonight" (7): it brings people into the town.

5. **The scarf comes from nowhere**
   Current: "A hat, a scarf, and a trail through snow"
   Why: the scarf was never set up, while the stolen hat (the strongest setup in the song) goes unpaid.
   Fix:
   a) "Just a stolen hat and a trail through snow" (9): it pays off the theft.
   b) "A hat that was not hers and a trail through snow" (11): a direct echo of the strongest line.

### Minor Notes

- **"fjord" pronunciation:** Suno may sing "ford" or "fee-ord". Listen on take A; if it fails, use "The pine trees bow and the lake ice breaks".
- **Bridge density:** 11-12 syllables per whispered line at 145 BPM. If Suno rushes them, drop "now" from line 1 and "out of snow" from line 3.
- **Meter risk:** the bed reads as 4/4 to the beat tracker. Check `time_signature` with `audio-analysis` and by ear on each take, and log it (SOP 10).
