## The Innuendo Architecture

How the song escalates:

| Section | Innuendo Level | Example |
|---|---|---|
| V1 | Subtle — physical description, first glances | "Licks the wooden spoon like she's casting a hex" / "catches me watching — grins nice and slow" |
| PC1 | Double-entendre | "Every time she bends down I forget what I wished" |
| V2 | Barely a metaphor | "Apron slipping off one shoulder, nothing underneath" |
| PC2 | Explicitly suggestive | "Then puts 'em somewhere else" |
| V3 | The aftermath — it already happened | "Handprints in the flour and her ripped-up dress" |
| Bridge | Drops the food metaphor entirely | "The way she pulls me closer / With her back against the door" |
| Outro | Pillow talk | "Round two? Or you want leftovers?" |

The CHORUS stays clean-ish (same hook) — it's the SINGALONG part. The verses do the dirty work. This is deliberate: the hook needs to be chantable without embarrassment at a concert. The verses are the wink.
